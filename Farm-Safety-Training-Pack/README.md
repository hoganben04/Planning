## How to use this pack

This pack is designed to be **worked through in order, with a supervisor**, before the trainee operates any machinery unsupervised. It mixes reading, discussion ("toolbox talks"), practical demonstration and sign-off.

**The rule is simple: read the module → discuss it with your supervisor → watch it demonstrated → do it under supervision → get signed off.** No one operates a machine alone until they are signed off as competent for that machine.

## A note on what this pack is and isn't

This pack is built on current **UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE)** guidance for agriculture and young people, plus recognised good practice. It is a strong, practical foundation — but it does **not** replace:

- **Accredited operator training** (e.g. **Lantra** or **City & Guilds NPTC**) for telehandlers/rough-terrain lift trucks, ATVs, chainsaws and pesticide application. These should be booked where the trainee will use that kit.
- A **site-specific risk assessment** for Truleigh Manor Farm (Module 12 is your template).
- The **operator's manual** for each individual machine — always the final word on that machine.

Keep the completed pack on file. It is your evidence that you provided information, instruction, training and supervision as the law requires.
