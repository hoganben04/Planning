# Module 02 — The Big Killers & Safe Stop

*The single most important module. If you remember nothing else, remember Safe Stop.*

---

## What actually kills and injures people on farms

Across recent years, HSE's fatal injury data for agriculture shows the same causes coming up again and again. In the 2023/24 reporting year there were around **23 agriculture-related deaths**. The main categories were:

| Cause | What it looks like in real life |
|---|---|
| **Transport** (the biggest) | Being **run over** by a tractor/telehandler, vehicles **overturning** on slopes, people **crushed** against walls/gates, runaway machines |
| **Struck by moving/falling objects** | **Falling bales**, collapsing stacks, loads dropping from loaders, raised trailer bodies coming down |
| **Livestock** | Crushed, knocked down or trampled (especially cattle, and cows with calves) |
| **Machinery contact** | **Entanglement in PTO shafts** and moving parts; reaching into running machines to clear blockages |
| **Falls from height** | Falling from machines, trailers, stacks, fragile roofs; falls while working off a telehandler |

**Notice the pattern:** almost all of these involve a machine that was *moving or could move*, or a load that could *fall*. And a large share happen during a quick "I'll just sort that" moment — clearing a blockage, hopping off to open a gate, nipping under a raised body.

> **HSE has found that around 60% of all "run over" deaths on farms could have been prevented simply by putting the handbrake on.** That is how powerful one habit is.

---

## SAFE STOP — the four steps

Do this **every single time** before you leave the operating position, before anyone approaches the machine, and before any adjustment, maintenance or blockage clearing:

### 1. Handbrake ON
### 2. Controls in NEUTRAL (and lower any raised attachment to the ground)
### 3. Engine OFF
### 4. Key OUT (and pocket it — or lock off the power)

A simple way to remember it: **"Brake — Neutral — Off — Out."**

If you're getting off even for ten seconds to open a gate, pick something up, or talk to someone: **Safe Stop.** Machines have rolled and killed people in the time it takes to walk round to the back.

---

## When you MUST Safe Stop

- Before getting off the seat for **any** reason.
- Before **anyone** approaches the machine or steps near moving parts.
- Before clearing a **blockage** (baler, mower, tedder — anything).
- Before any **adjustment, greasing, refuelling or maintenance**.
- Before checking, touching, or photographing anything on the machine.
- Before walking away from it — even "just for a minute."

## The deadly shortcut to never take

The classic fatal accident: a machine blocks, the operator leaves it running ("it'll only take a second"), reaches in, and a sleeve, glove or hand is caught by a still-turning part. **PTO shafts spin fast and have no mercy.** Moving parts can keep turning for several seconds after you disengage drive. **Stop everything. Wait until ALL movement has completely stopped. Then clear it.**

---

## Parking up safely

- Park on **firm, level ground** where you can.
- Lower all attachments (loader, forks, linkage) **to the ground**.
- Handbrake on, in gear/park as the manual says, chock wheels if on any slope.
- Never leave a machine **running and unattended**.
- Never leave a machine where it could roll, or where children could reach it.

---

## Discussion / demonstration

- Recite the four Safe Stop steps from memory.
- Demonstrate Safe Stop on a tractor to your supervisor.
- Explain *why* you wait for moving parts to stop before clearing a blockage, even after disengaging the PTO.
