# Module 11 — Knowledge Check Answer Key

*For the supervisor. ★ = safety-critical: the trainee must get these right before unsupervised work. Re-teach anything missed.*

---

### Section A
1. Heavy machinery, working alone, vehicles, livestock, working at height, time pressure; fatal injury rate ~21× the all-industry average.
2. Any three: transport (run-over/overturn/crush), being struck by moving/falling objects (e.g. bales), livestock, machinery entanglement (incl. PTO), falls from height.
3. **★ Ask your supervisor, and be trained and signed off as competent — never use a machine you haven't been trained on.**
4. True. No task is worth a serious injury; if unsure or unsafe, stop and ask.
5. Any three: first aid kit, fire extinguishers, assembly point, overhead power lines, fuel/chemical stores, farm address for 999, welfare facilities, supervisor contact.

### Section B ★
6. **★ 1) Handbrake on  2) Controls in neutral  3) Engine off  4) Key out.** ("Brake–Neutral–Off–Out.")
7. **★ Any three:** before leaving the seat; before anyone approaches; before clearing a blockage; before any adjustment/maintenance/refuelling/greasing; before walking away.
8. **★** Safe Stop (handbrake, neutral, PTO disengaged, engine off, key out and pocketed) → **wait for all moving parts to fully stop** → clear with a tool, watching for stored energy → replace guard → stand clear → restart.
9. **★** Moving parts (flywheels, rotors, tines) **coast on for several seconds** after drive is disengaged and can still entangle/injure.
10. About **60%**.

### Section C
11. **Category F licence**; tractor up to **2.45 m wide** (and trailer ≤ 2.45 m, two wheels or four close-coupled). Width limit lifts at 17.
12. **★** Driving across a slope risks a **sideways overturn**; up/down keeps the tractor's centre of gravity stable.
13. **★** Hitching high makes the tractor **rear up and roll over backwards** when it pulls; always use the drawbar/proper hitch at the correct (low) height.
14. Two hands + one foot (or two feet + one hand) in contact when getting on/off; used every time mounting/dismounting — never jump.
15. **Stay in**, hold on, lean away from the fall. The cab/ROPS + seatbelt protect you; jumping out means being crushed.
16. Any three: tyres, fluids/leaks, lights/beacon, PTO and guards, brakes, seatbelt, steps/handholds, hitch/linkage.
17. **False** — no passengers in/on trailers, ever. They can fall and be run over/crushed.

### Section D ★
18. **★ Yes, but only on private land, and only if trained (ideally accredited), demonstrably competent and mature, AND adequately supervised.** Never on a public road at 16.
19. **★** A raised/extended load raises the centre of gravity and the machine **tips** (sideways or forwards), especially turning or on slopes.
20. **★** The **load chart / rated capacity indicator**. Capacity **drops sharply** as the boom is raised and extended forward.
21. **★** Bales can **roll back** down the loader arms toward the cab/operator; use a bale spike or grab designed for the job.
22. **★** Stay in the cab; if possible drive clear of the line; warn everyone to keep back and not touch the machine; call 999 and the network emergency line (105). Only if forced out by fire: **jump clear feet together, don't touch machine and ground at once, shuffle away**.
23. Only in a **purpose-made, tested man-basket/cage rated for that telehandler** — never on the bare forks.

### Section E ★
24. **★** The Power Take-Off shaft transfers engine power to the implement, spinning at 540/1000 rpm; it **entangles clothing/limbs** and kills/dismembers. It must be fully guarded with anti-rotation chains.
25. **★** Loose items can be **caught by rotating parts** and drag the person in. Tie up/remove; tuck in clothing; tie back hair.
26. **★** **Take the machine out of use and report it — do NOT run it.** Replace/repair the guard before use.
27. Energy held in tensioned springs, hydraulic pressure, raised parts or a loaded pick-up reel that can spring back when released; e.g. a baler pick-up under tension, a raised mower.
28. **Stand well clear of the rear** — an ejected bale and the tailgate can strike/crush you.

### Section F ★
29. Mow → ted/turn → rake (row up) → bale → (wrap, for haylage) → cart → stack.
30. **★** Build square, **bonded/overlapping**, on firm level ground, not too tall for the base, away from lines/edges; **never climb it** — falls and collapses kill; take stacks down top-down.
31. **★** Hot/"burning caramel" smell, steam, stack hot to a probe; **tell supervisor immediately, do not disturb the hot stack** (adding air can ignite it), be ready to call 999.
32. They contain **more moisture**, so they're heavier; this must be within the telehandler's **load limits** for the boom position used.
33. Fatigue slows reactions and judgement; rushing in poor light causes mistakes — most accidents cluster in long, pressured days.

### Section G
34. **★** Stable base, close to load, **bend knees not back**, secure grip, lift with legs, keep load close, **don't twist**, set down with knees. Use a machine/help for heavy loads.
35. (a) Noisy machines / when you'd raise your voice to be heard; (b) dusty or mouldy hay/dust; (c) tedding/mowing/grinding or anything that could flick into eyes.
36. A serious, sometimes permanent lung disease from breathing **mould spores** in hay; avoid by wearing an FFP3 mask, working upwind, not disturbing mouldy bales unmasked.
37. **★** Roofs (fibre/asbestos cement, rooflights) are **fragile** — you fall straight through. Specialist job only.
38. **★** Risk of **toxic gas / no oxygen** (slurry gas kills in seconds) and **engulfment/suffocation** in grain. Confined-space entry needs permits, testing and rescue — never for an inexperienced young worker.
39. Calm and quiet, keep an escape route, never get cornered or between a cow and calf, treat bulls as dangerous, don't enter alone if inexperienced.

### Section H ★
40. **★** Make it safe without becoming a second casualty (Safe Stop, isolate power); check the casualty; **call 999/112 — give postcode + what3words/grid ref, what happened, how many hurt, injuries**; give first aid within training (recovery position / control bleeding / CPR if trained); **send someone to the gate/road to guide the ambulance in**; report to supervisor.

---

**Marking note:** Treat any ★ question answered wrongly as a re-teach before the trainee works unsupervised on the relevant task. Record completion in Module 14.
