# Module 10 — Knowledge Check (Quiz)

**Trainee name:** _________________________   **Date:** __________

Complete this **after** working through Modules 01–09. Answer in your own words / circle the answer. Your supervisor will mark it using Module 11. The **safety-critical questions are marked ★ — you must get these right.** Anything missed will be re-taught before you work unsupervised.

---

## Section A — Farm safety basics

1. Why is farming considered one of the most dangerous industries in the UK?

2. Name **three** of the main causes of death on UK farms.

3. ★ List the **two** most important things you must do before you operate any machine you haven't been trained on. *(Clue: it involves asking and being signed off.)*

4. True or false: "There's no job on the farm urgent enough to be worth getting hurt for." Explain.

5. Name three things you should locate during your first-day induction.

---

## Section B — Safe Stop & the big killers ★

6. ★ Write out the **four steps of Safe Stop**, in order.

7. ★ Name **three** situations where you must perform Safe Stop.

8. ★ A baler is blocked. Describe exactly what you do, step by step, before clearing it.

9. ★ Why do you wait after switching off, even though you've disengaged the PTO?

10. Roughly what percentage of run-over deaths on farms could be prevented just by using the handbrake?

---

## Section C — Tractors

11. At 16, on a public road, what licence do you need to drive a tractor, and what width limit applies?

12. ★ Why do you drive **up and down** a slope rather than across it?

13. ★ Why must you never hitch a load high up on the tractor instead of at the drawbar?

14. What is "three points of contact" and when do you use it?

15. If a tractor with a cab and seatbelt starts to overturn, should you jump out or stay in? Why?

16. Name three things you check in a pre-start walk-round.

17. True or false: it's fine to give a mate a lift in the trailer across the field. Explain.

---

## Section D — Telehandlers & loaders ★

18. ★ Can a 16-year-old operate a telehandler? Under what conditions?

19. ★ Why must you travel with the load **low** and the boom retracted?

20. ★ What document tells you the maximum safe load, and what happens to capacity as you raise and extend the boom?

21. ★ Why must you never use the loader bucket to move round bales?

22. ★ The telehandler boom contacts an overhead power line. What do you do?

23. Can you lift a person on the forks? Explain.

---

## Section E — PTO & machinery ★

24. ★ What is the PTO shaft and why is it so dangerous?

25. ★ Why must loose clothing, cords and jewellery be tied up or removed around machinery?

26. ★ A guard is broken. What do you do? Can you run the machine?

27. What is "stored energy" and give one example on a baler or mower.

28. Where should you stand when a round bale is being ejected from the baler? Why?

---

## Section F — Hay & haylage ★

29. List the stages of making hay/haylage from field to stack.

30. ★ How do you build a bale stack so it doesn't collapse, and why must you never climb on it?

31. ★ What are the warning signs that stacked hay is heating up, and what must you do?

32. Why are haylage bales heavier than hay bales, and why does that matter for the telehandler?

33. Why are long days and "one more load in the dark" a particular danger?

---

## Section G — Health, PPE & site hazards

34. ★ Describe how to lift a heavy object correctly.

35. When must you wear (a) ear protection (b) a dust mask (c) eye protection?

36. What is "Farmer's Lung" and how do you avoid it?

37. ★ Why must you never walk on an old farm building roof?

38. ★ Why must you never enter a slurry tank or grain store?

39. How should you behave around cattle, especially cows with calves?

---

## Section H — Emergencies ★

40. ★ Someone is seriously injured in the far field. Describe what you do, in order, including exactly what information you give to 999 and how you help the ambulance find the farm.
