# A2 — Greasing & Daily Operator Maintenance

*Shared procedure for all machines. Greasing and daily checks are the maintenance jobs a young
operator does — and most maintenance deaths happen because a machine was running, or moved, while
someone worked on it. Get the safety routine right every time.*

---

## The golden rule of any maintenance: SAFE STOP first

Before you grease, check, or touch any machine — even for "just a second":

1. **Handbrake on**
2. **Controls in neutral; lower all attachments** (loader, forks, linkage, mower beds) **to the ground**
3. **Engine off**
4. **Key out and in your pocket** (so no one can start it while you're at the sharp end)
5. **Wait** for any rotating part to **completely stop**

If you must work under something raised (rare — avoid it), it **must be physically propped or locked**
— never trust hydraulics to hold it up.

---

## Greasing — how and where

Grease keeps pins, pivots and bearings from seizing and wearing out. A grease gun pushes grease
through a **nipple** into the joint.

**How to use a grease gun:**
- Wipe the **grease nipple** clean first so you don't push grit in.
- Push the coupler squarely onto the nipple; pump until you feel/see fresh grease reach the joint
  (a little squeezing out is normal — wipe the excess).
- If a nipple won't take grease, **don't force it** — tell your supervisor (it may be blocked or the
  joint damaged).
- Use the **correct grease**; don't mix random types.

**Typical points to grease (check each machine's page and manual for the exact list):**

| Area | Examples |
|---|---|
| **PTO shafts** | Universal joints at each end, the sliding inner tube, and the guard bearings — these are high-wear and often missed |
| **Three-point linkage / drawbar** | Pins, top-link, lift arms, pickup hitch |
| **Loader / telehandler** | Boom pivot pins, ram pin ends, headstock/quick-hitch, axle pivots |
| **Tedder / rake** | Rotor gearbox arms, wheel pivots, folding rams' pins |
| **Mower** | Cutterbar (per manual), pivot points, breakback pivot |
| **Baler** | Many points — pickup, plunger area, knotter (per manual/auto-lube), bale chamber rollers |
| **Trailer** | Hitch eye, brake linkages, tipping ram pins, axle points |

> Many balers and bigger machines have **auto-lube** or grease banks (a row of nipples on one block)
> — your supervisor will show you the bank and the schedule.

## Daily checks (do before first use each day)

A quick walk-round catches most faults before they bite:

- [ ] **Oil** — engine oil on the dipstick within marks (engine cold/level ground).
- [ ] **Coolant** — level in the expansion tank (only check when **cold** — never open a hot cap).
- [ ] **Hydraulic oil** — sight glass/dipstick level.
- [ ] **Diesel & AdBlue** — enough for the job (see A1).
- [ ] **Tyres** — condition, no big cuts, pressures look right, wheel nuts present.
- [ ] **Leaks** — look under the machine for oil/coolant/hydraulic/fuel pooling.
- [ ] **Guards** — all present and secure, especially **PTO guards and chains**.
- [ ] **Lights, beacon, indicators, brake lights, mirrors, horn** — clean and working.
- [ ] **Steps, handholds, glass** — clean of mud (slip prevention / visibility).
- [ ] **Attachment / implement** — pins, clips and quick-hitch locked.

---

## After maintenance — before you restart

- **Replace every guard** you removed.
- Remove any tools, rags or grease guns from the machine.
- Make sure **no one is near** moving parts.
- Then, and only then, start up.

---

## Report defects — don't just live with them

If something is worn, leaking, loose, missing a guard, or "not quite right":

- **Take the machine out of use** if it's a safety item (brakes, guards, steering, hitch).
- **Tell your supervisor** and, if the farm has one, write it in the **defect/maintenance log**.
- Never "make do" with a broken guard or a dodgy brake to get the job finished.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Safe Stop + key in pocket before greasing | Grease/adjust with the engine running |
| Wait for all parts to stop | Reach into a coasting machine |
| Wipe nipples, use correct grease | Force grease into a blocked nipple |
| Prop/lock anything raised you work under | Trust hydraulics to hold a load up |
| Replace guards before restarting | Run a machine with a guard off |
| Report and log defects | "Make do" with a known fault |

**Sign-off:** Trainee can carry out daily checks and grease a machine safely (Safe Stop, correct points, guards replaced).
