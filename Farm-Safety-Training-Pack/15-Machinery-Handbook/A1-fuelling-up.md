# A1 — Fuelling Up (Diesel & AdBlue)

*Shared procedure for all tractors and telehandlers. Refuelling is routine — which is exactly why
people get careless. Diesel is a fire and environmental hazard, and a slip/trip hazard around the
fuel store.*

---

## Before you fuel

- **Engine OFF. Safe Stop** (handbrake, neutral, key out). Never refuel a running engine.
- **No smoking, no naked flames, no phones** at the fuel store or pump.
- Park on **firm, level ground** by the fuel store.
- Know where the **spill kit** and **fire extinguisher** are at the fuel store.
- Wear **gloves**; avoid breathing fumes; don't get diesel on your skin or clothes.

---

## Diesel — the routine

1. Open the fuel store / bowser as instructed; check the **nozzle and hose** are sound.
2. Wipe around the filler cap so dirt doesn't drop into the tank; remove the cap.
3. Put the nozzle **fully into the filler** and keep hold of it the whole time — **don't walk away**.
4. Fill **steadily**; listen and watch for the level. **Stop before it overflows** — don't "top off"
   to the brim, especially on a hot day (diesel expands and will overflow).
5. Replace the cap firmly. Wipe up any drips with the spill kit/rag.
6. Log the fuel taken if the farm keeps a fuel book.

> **Static electricity** can ignite fuel vapour. Keep the nozzle in contact with the filler neck
> while filling. Never fill a container on the back of a plastic-lined truck bed.

---

## AdBlue / DEF (Puma 240 CVX, 6150R, modern telehandlers)

Modern diesel engines use **AdBlue** (a urea solution, also called DEF) in a **separate blue-capped
tank** to clean the exhaust. It is **not fuel** and must never go in the diesel tank — and diesel
must never go in the AdBlue tank. Getting them mixed up causes expensive engine/exhaust damage.

- Use the **blue AdBlue filler** only, with the correct nozzle.
- AdBlue is mildly corrosive and crystallises — **wipe up spills with water** and rinse, don't leave
  white deposits on paint/connectors.
- Keep it clean — dirt in AdBlue blocks the system. Don't decant it through a dirty funnel.
- Wash hands after handling.

---

## Spills & the environment

A diesel spill that reaches a ditch, drain or watercourse is a **pollution offence** and can kill
fish and wildlife — and Truleigh Manor has brooks, lakes and water features (see the field map).

- **Contain any spill immediately** with the spill kit (granules/socks). Don't hose it into a drain.
- Tell your supervisor about **every** spill, however small.
- Never refuel right next to a ditch, pond or drain if it can be avoided.

---

## Fuel store good practice

- Keep the store **locked** and the area free of trip hazards and clutter.
- Report low fuel, leaks, or a damaged hose/nozzle.
- Don't fill jerry cans unless shown how, and only in **approved metal/plastic fuel containers**,
  on the ground, never in a vehicle.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Engine off, Safe Stop, before fuelling | Refuel a running or hot-restart engine |
| Hold the nozzle the whole time | Walk away mid-fill |
| Stop before the brim | Overfill / top off on a hot day |
| Use the blue filler for AdBlue only | Mix diesel and AdBlue |
| Contain spills, tell supervisor | Hose fuel toward a ditch or drain |
