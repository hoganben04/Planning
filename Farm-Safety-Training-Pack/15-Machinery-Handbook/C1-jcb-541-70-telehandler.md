# C1 — JCB 541-70 Loadall (Telehandler)

*Read [Module 04 — Telehandlers](../04-telehandlers-and-loaders.md) first — the law, training and
stability rules there are essential. This page adds the JCB 541-70 specifics.*

> **Reminder:** a 16-year-old may use this **only on private farmland**, only if **trained
> (accredited Lantra/NPTC), competent, mature AND adequately supervised** — and **not on a public
> road**. This is our biggest, longest-reach machine; treat it with the most respect.

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Agricultural telehandler (Loadall) |
| **Max lift capacity** | **~4,100 kg (4.1 t)** |
| **Max lift height** | **~7.0 m** |
| Capacity falls to | **~1.5 t at full forward reach**, ~2.25 t at full height |
| Machine weight | ~8.2 t |
| Fuel | Diesel (+ AdBlue on newer models) |

**The key safety fact:** the load it can lift **drops sharply as the boom goes up and out**. 4.1 t is
only with the boom low and retracted. Always work to the **load chart in the cab**.

## Pre-start daily checks

[A2 daily checks](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md), plus:

- [ ] Tyres (big, high-pressure) and wheel nuts.
- [ ] **Boom, hoses, rams** — no leaks, no damage, no cracks.
- [ ] **Quick-hitch / headstock**: attachment (bale spike or grab) **locked and pinned**.
- [ ] **Load chart** present and readable; **boom/load stability indicator** working.
- [ ] Brakes, steering, **reversing alarm**, beacon, horn, lights, mirrors/camera.
- [ ] **Seatbelt** — wear it.
- [ ] Forks/attachment correct for the job (see [E1](E1-bale-grabs-and-squeeze.md)).

## Safe operation

**Start-up**
- Three points of contact in; **seatbelt on**; familiarise yourself with the joystick functions.

**Picking up & travelling**
- Approach the load square; engage the attachment fully.
- **Lift, then tilt back slightly** to cradle the load.
- **Carry LOW** — boom down and retracted — whenever travelling. A raised load while moving is the
  classic way to tip a telehandler.
- Travel and **turn slowly**; the machine is long and the back end swings wide.

**Lifting & placing**
- Position on **firm, level ground**, square to the stack.
- Lift smoothly; **watch the load chart and stability indicator** — stop before any warning.
- **Never extend beyond the chart** for the height/reach you're at.
- Keep **everyone out of the working area** — nobody under or near a raised load.
- Place on firm ground, lower, release, **reverse out straight**.

**Overhead power lines (a telehandler killer)**
- The 7 m boom can reach lines a tractor never would, and **electricity can arc across a gap**.
- **Know where every line on the farm runs** (mark them on the [field map](../16-Farm-Map-and-Field-Names.md)).
- Plan stacking/tipping **well clear** of lines. If you contact one: **stay in the cab**, reverse/
  lower clear if possible, keep everyone back, call for help (Module 08).

**Slopes**
- Keep the **load on the uphill side**, travel up/down, load low. Avoid lifting on a slope.

**Never**
- Never lift a person on the bare forks — only in a **purpose-made, tested cage** rated for this
  machine.
- Never use the machine beyond the load chart "to save a second."

## Fuelling
Engine off, Safe Stop, no smoking. Diesel (and AdBlue if fitted). See [A1](A1-fuelling-up.md).

## Greasing & operator maintenance
Safe Stop, **boom down on the ground**, key in pocket. Grease the **boom pivot pins, ram pin ends,
the headstock/quick-hitch, and axle/steering pivots** — there are many pins; follow the JCB grease
schedule and bank. Check hydraulic oil, leaks, tyres, and that the load chart is legible.
See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

## Shutdown / parking
Firm level ground, **attachment flat on the ground, boom fully retracted and lowered**, handbrake
on, engine off, key out. Never park with the boom up or a load raised.

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Work to the load chart | Guess the weight / over-reach |
| Travel with the load LOW, boom in | Drive with a raised load |
| Turn and travel slowly | Swing the long back end into things/people |
| Plan work clear of power lines | Raise the boom near overhead lines |
| Lift people only in a tested cage | Lift anyone on bare forks |
| Park boom down, attachment on the ground | Leave the boom raised |
