# D1 — Kuhn GF 8501 (Rotary Tedder)

*Read [Module 05 — PTO & field machinery](../05-pto-and-machinery.md) first. The tedder spreads and
turns cut grass to dry it. It's a wide machine with fast-spinning tine rotors.*

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Mounted rotary tedder, **~8.5 m working width**, multiple rotors |
| Driven by | Tractor **PTO** (typically 540 rpm) |
| Main hazards | Fast spinning tines flinging debris; **folding wings** (crush points); transport width; tines that **coast** after the PTO stops |

---

## Pre-start daily checks

[A2 daily checks](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md) on the tractor, plus on the tedder:

- [ ] **Tines** — present, not bent/broken/missing (flying tines are dangerous). Replace as needed.
- [ ] **PTO shaft** — full guard + anti-rotation chains, both ends locked on.
- [ ] **Folding rams/locks** — sound; hoses not chafed.
- [ ] Rotor gearboxes/arms — no obvious damage; greased (see below).
- [ ] Transport latches/locks for the road.

---

## Safe operation

**Hitching**
- Hitch on firm, level ground, engine off when connecting the **PTO shaft**; check guard and chains.
- Connect hydraulics for folding; know which lever folds/unfolds.

**Unfolding / folding**
- **Keep everyone clear of the folding path** — the wings are heavy crush hazards.
- Fold/unfold exactly as the manual shows; engage **transport locks** before the road.

**In work**
- Engage PTO at **low revs**, build up gently.
- The tines **throw grass, stones and debris** — keep bystanders well clear; wear **eye protection**
  when working near it.
- Watch your **8.5 m width** — through gateways, near hedges, fences and the sand fencing (see field
  map), and around field obstacles.
- On slopes and rough ground, go at a sensible speed.

**Blockages / tangled grass**
- **Safe Stop** (PTO disengaged, engine off, key out) and **wait for the rotors to stop** — they
  coast for several seconds. Then clear, using a tool, watching for spring-back. Never clear it
  running (Module 05).

**Transport**
- Folded, locked, within legal width/height; lights/markers visible; beacon on.

---

## Greasing & operator maintenance
Safe Stop, key in pocket. Grease the **rotor arm pivots, wheel/axle pivots, and the folding ram
pins**, plus the **PTO shaft joints and sliding tube** — to Kuhn's schedule. Check tines and guards
before every use. See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

## Shutdown / parking
Lower to the ground (or park folded as designed) on firm level ground; Safe Stop.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Eye protection; keep bystanders clear | Stand near spinning/throwing tines |
| Keep clear of folding wings | Be under a folding wing |
| Wait for rotors to stop before clearing | Unblock with the PTO turning |
| Mind the 8.5 m width & transport locks | Travel unfolded/unlatched on the road |
| Replace bent/broken tines | Run with missing/damaged tines |
