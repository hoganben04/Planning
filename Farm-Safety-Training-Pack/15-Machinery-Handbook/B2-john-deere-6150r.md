# B2 — John Deere 6150R (Tractor)

*Read [Module 03 — Tractors](../03-tractors.md) first. This page adds the points specific to the
John Deere 6150R.*

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Mid-large tractor, ~150 hp |
| Transmission | Powershift / AutoQuad-type (depending on spec) — know whether yours has a clutch pedal and how the ranges work |
| Fuel | Diesel **+ AdBlue/DEF** (blue cap) — see [A1](A1-fuelling-up.md) |
| Typical jobs here | Tedding, raking, front + rear mowing, carting bales, general work |

> Confirm your exact transmission type and control layout with your supervisor — Deere offers
> several. Learn **where every control is before you move**.

## Pre-start daily checks

Do the [A2 daily checks](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md), plus on the 6150R:

- [ ] Diesel **and AdBlue** levels.
- [ ] Transmission/hydraulic oil level.
- [ ] Front linkage & front PTO (for the front mower) — pins, guard, hydraulics.
- [ ] **Seatbelt** and **ROPS/cab** sound; fire extinguisher present.
- [ ] Tyres and any ballast/wheel weights suited to the implement.

## Safe operation

**Start-up**
- Three points of contact in; set seat suspension; **seatbelt on**.
- Start from the seat only, transmission in neutral/park, PTO off.
- Watch the display for warnings (AdBlue/DEF, DPF/regen, hydraulics).

**Driving**
- Move off smoothly; select range/speed appropriate to the task.
- **Handbrake/park at every stop.**
- Know how the **hitch/SCV (spool) controls** work before connecting implements and hydraulics.

**In work (PTO machines — tedder, rake, mower, baler)**
- Set the correct **PTO speed** (540/1000) for the implement.
- Engage PTO at **low revs** and build up; never at full throttle.
- For the **front + rear mower combination**, both front and rear PTOs run — keep everyone well
  clear of both ends (see [D4](D4-kuhn-front-rear-3m-mowers.md)).

**Transport / road**
- Brake pedals **latched together** for road.
- Mind width/length through our gateways; beacon on; follow Module 03 road rules.

**Slopes**
- Up/down not across, slow, no sharp turns, especially with a loader or full kit. Extra care on the
  steeper ground (see field map).

## Fuelling & AdBlue
Engine off, Safe Stop, no smoking. Diesel in the main filler, **AdBlue in the blue filler only**.
Don't let AdBlue run dry. See [A1](A1-fuelling-up.md).

## Greasing & operator maintenance
Safe Stop, key in pocket. Grease **front and rear linkage pins, top link, pickup hitch, loader pins
if fitted, and the PTO shaft joints + sliding tube** of the implement in use. Daily oil/coolant
(cold)/hydraulic/tyre checks; guards on. See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

## Shutdown / parking
Firm level ground, lower linkage/loader, neutral/park, **handbrake on, engine off, key out**.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Learn the controls before moving | Guess which lever does what under load |
| Seatbelt + handbrake every stop | Rely on transmission to hold on a slope |
| Front + rear mower: keep clear of both ends | Stand near a running front mower |
| AdBlue in blue filler only | Mix AdBlue and diesel |
| Gentle PTO engagement | Engage PTO at full throttle |
