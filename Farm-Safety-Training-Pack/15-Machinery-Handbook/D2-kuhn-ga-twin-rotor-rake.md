# D2 — Kuhn GA Twin Rotor Rake

*Read [Module 05 — PTO & field machinery](../05-pto-and-machinery.md) first. The rake gathers the
dried/wilted grass into rows ("windrows") ready for the baler. Two rotors with sprung tine arms
sweep the grass together.*

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Trailed/mounted **twin-rotor rake** |
| Driven by | Tractor **PTO** + hydraulics for lift/fold |
| Main hazards | **Rotating tine arms** at body height; **folding rotors** (crush + overhead reach); tines that coast on; transport width/height |

---

## Pre-start daily checks

[A2 daily checks](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md), plus:

- [ ] **Tine arms & tines** — secure, none bent/broken/missing; **tine guards** in place.
- [ ] **PTO shaft** — full guard + chains, locked both ends.
- [ ] **Folding rams, hoses, locks** — sound.
- [ ] Rotor height/working width set correctly.

---

## Safe operation

**Hitching & folding**
- Connect PTO with the **engine off**; check guard/chains.
- When **folding the rotors up**, they swing **wide and high** — keep clear of the folding path, and
  **watch for overhead power lines** when rotors are raised for transport (see field map / Module 08).

**In work**
- Engage PTO at **low revs**.
- The **tine arms sweep round at body height** — never reach in or stand close while it turns.
- Keep bystanders clear; thrown debris and a flicked tine can injure.
- Mind ground speed on slopes and around obstacles.

**Blockages / wrapped grass**
- **Safe Stop**, **wait for the rotors and tine arms to stop** (they coast), then clear with a tool.
  Watch for sprung tine arms snapping back. Never clear it running.

**Transport**
- Rotors folded and **locked**; within legal width/height; lights/markers; beacon on.

---

## Greasing & operator maintenance
Safe Stop, key in pocket. Grease the **rotor bearings/cam track points, tine-arm pivots, wheel
pivots and folding ram pins** to Kuhn's schedule, plus the **PTO shaft**. Check tines, guards and
folding locks. See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

## Shutdown / parking
Lower rotors to the ground on firm level ground (or park folded as designed); Safe Stop.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Stand well clear of the turning tine arms | Reach toward a turning rotor |
| Watch power lines when rotors are raised | Fold rotors up under a power line |
| Wait for everything to stop before clearing | Pull wrapped grass out while it runs |
| Transport folded & locked | Travel with rotors unlatched |
