# D4 — Kuhn Front & Rear 3 m Mowers (Disc Mower Combination)

*Read [Module 05 — PTO & field machinery](../05-pto-and-machinery.md) first. We run a **front-mounted
3 m mower** and a **rear-mounted 3 m mower** together (a "combination", ~6 m cut). Disc mowers have
razor-sharp discs/blades spinning extremely fast — the biggest "thrown object" risk on the farm.*

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Front + rear mounted **disc mowers**, ~3 m each (~6 m combined) |
| Driven by | **Front PTO** (front mower) **and rear PTO** (rear mower) — both running together |
| Main hazards | **Razor-sharp fast discs**; **thrown stones/objects/broken blades** (can travel a long way); two cutting units front and rear; discs that **coast** after the PTO stops; folding for transport |

---

## Pre-start daily checks

[A2 daily checks](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md), plus on **each** mower:

- [ ] **Blades/knives** — present, sharp, not cracked or worn thin; **replace damaged blades as a set**
  with the correct bolts/pins (a flying blade is lethal).
- [ ] **Disc/skid condition** and cutterbar — no damage.
- [ ] **Protective curtains/guards/covers** around the cutterbar — **all in place** (these stop
  thrown objects).
- [ ] **PTO shafts (front and rear)** — full guards + chains, locked on.
- [ ] **Breakback** device free (lets the mower swing back if it hits an obstacle).
- [ ] Folding rams/locks and hoses sound.

---

## Safe operation

**Before cutting — walk the field**
- Disc mowers throw whatever they hit. **Check the field for stones, debris, wire, posts and hidden
  obstacles**, and for people/animals, before you start.

**Hitching**
- Connect **front and rear PTOs** with the **engine off**; check both guards and chains.

**In work**
- Engage PTO at **low revs**, build up gently. Both units come up to speed.
- **Keep everyone well clear — front AND rear, and especially out of the line of throw.** Never let
  anyone stand in front of, beside, or behind the mowers while running.
- Lower the cutterbars only when clear; raise at headlands.
- Mind the ~6 m total width and the front unit's reach in gateways and near hedges/fences.
- On slopes, go steady (Module 03).

**Hitting an obstacle**
- If a mower hits something, the **breakback** lets it swing back — **stop, Safe Stop, and inspect**
  for damaged blades before continuing. A cracked blade can fly off.

**Blockages / clearing grass**
- **Safe Stop** (both PTOs disengaged, engine off, key out) and **wait for the discs to STOP** — they
  coast for several seconds and the blades are razor-sharp. Then clear with a tool and gloves.
  Never near a running or coasting disc.

**Transport**
- Fold and **lock** as designed; guards/curtains down; within legal width/height; lights/beacon.

---

## Greasing & operator maintenance
Safe Stop, key in pocket, **discs stopped**. Grease **pivot points, folding ram pins and the
front + rear PTO shaft joints/sliding tubes**; check the **cutterbar oil level** per Kuhn's manual.
**Always replace the protective curtains/guards before running.** Inspect blades and fixings daily.
See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

## Shutdown / parking
Lower cutterbars to the ground on firm level ground (or park folded as designed); Safe Stop.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Walk the field for stones/debris first | Mow over unknown debris/wire |
| Keep everyone clear, front and rear, out of throw line | Let anyone stand near running mowers |
| Guards/curtains in place before running | Run with a curtain/guard removed |
| Replace damaged blades as a set | Carry on with a cracked blade |
| Wait for discs to fully stop before clearing | Touch a coasting/running disc |
| Fold & lock for transport | Travel unfolded/unlatched |
