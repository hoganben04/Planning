# E2 — Bale Trailers

*Read [Module 03 — Tractors (Trailers section)](../03-tractors.md) first. Bale trailers carry the
finished bales from field to stack. The dangers are an **unstable load**, **bales falling**, **people
riding or standing in the wrong place**, and **hitching/tipping** mistakes.*

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | Flat / bale trailer, towed behind the tractor |
| Main hazards | Load **toppling or sliding**; **bales falling** when loading/unloading/in transit; **runaway** trailer; **overturning** (top-heavy load, slopes, speed); crush while **hitching**; tipping into **overhead lines** |

---

## Before use

- [ ] **Hitch / drawbar eye** sound; correct size for the tractor pickup hitch.
- [ ] **Brakes** (if braked) and **lights/indicators** working — connect the lines.
- [ ] **Breakaway cable / safety chain** present and connected.
- [ ] Tyres and wheel nuts good; bed clear of loose debris.
- [ ] Within the **load rating** of trailer and tractor — don't overload.

---

## Hitching (a crush danger)

- Reverse up squarely; **Safe Stop** before getting between tractor and trailer.
- **Never stand between the tractor and trailer while the engine is running.** Many people are
  crushed at the hitch.
- Lock the hitch, fit the **safety pin/clip**, connect **brakes, lights and breakaway cable**, raise
  the jack/stand.

---

## Loading & securing

- Load **evenly and low** where possible; build the load **stable and bonded** so it can't shift or
  topple — like stacking (Module 06).
- Don't build the load **too high** for the trailer's width — top-heavy loads overturn.
- **Secure the load** as required (straps/net) so nothing falls in transit.
- **Round bales roll** — load so they can't roll off; chock if needed.
- Keep **clear of the trailer** while the telehandler loads it — never stand on or beside the bed in
  the loading zone (falling bales).

---

## Towing

- **No passengers** in or on the trailer or load — **ever**.
- Drive to suit the load: **slow on bends, slopes and rough ground**; a loaded bale trailer is heavy
  and can push or overturn the tractor.
- Allow extra **braking distance**; check brakes early on a hill.
- Mind **width, length and height** through our gateways and lanes (see field map), and watch for
  **overhead lines** with a high load.
- Take special care on the steeper ground (Sands Hill, The Hill).

---

## Unloading / tipping

- Unload on **firm, level ground**.
- If the trailer tips: tip only on level ground, **look up for overhead power lines first**, keep
  people clear, and **never go under a raised body** — prop it if you must work near it.
- Watch for the load **shifting or rolling** as straps come off — stand clear.

---

## Parking
- Park on firm, level ground; chock the wheels; lower the jack/stand; if unhitched, ensure it can't
  roll. Safe Stop the tractor.

---

## Greasing & maintenance
Grease the **hitch eye, brake linkages, tipping ram pins and axle points**; check brakes, lights and
tyres. Report worn hitches, dud lights or poor brakes — these are safety-critical. See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Connect brakes, lights, breakaway cable | Tow without lights/brakes/safety chain |
| Safe Stop before going to the hitch | Stand between tractor & trailer running |
| Load even, low, stable, secured | Build a tall top-heavy load |
| Keep clear while it's being loaded | Stand on the bed during loading |
| Look up for lines before tipping | Tip under/near overhead power lines |
| Slow on slopes & bends | Carry passengers on the trailer/load |
