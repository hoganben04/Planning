# Module 15 — Machinery Handbook

**Truleigh Manor Farm — safe operation & maintenance of our kit**

This is the machine-by-machine reference for the equipment used in our hay and haylage operation.
It builds on the general machine modules — read those **first**, because the rules in them apply to
everything here:

- **Module 02 — Safe Stop** (handbrake → neutral → engine off → key out)
- **Module 03 — Tractors**
- **Module 04 — Telehandlers**
- **Module 05 — PTO & field machinery**

---

## Three rules that apply to every machine in this handbook

1. **The operator's manual is the final word.** This handbook gives the safe routine and the key
   numbers; the manual in the cab gives the exact figures, intervals and warnings for that machine.
   If they ever disagree, follow the manual and tell your supervisor.
2. **Only operate a machine you have been trained and signed off on** (Module 13). Being shown once
   is not being signed off.
3. **Safe Stop before you leave the seat, before anyone approaches, and before you touch anything**
   — for checks, fuelling, greasing, clearing a blockage, or adjusting.

## What this handbook does NOT cover

This is an **operator's safety handbook**, not a workshop service manual. It deliberately does not
give engine internals, exact torque values, fluid grades or full service schedules — those come from
each machine's manufacturer manual and are the job of an experienced mechanic or the dealer. Your
maintenance role as a young operator is **daily checks, fuelling and greasing** done safely.
