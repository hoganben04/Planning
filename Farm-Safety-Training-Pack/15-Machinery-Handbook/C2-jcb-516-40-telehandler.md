# C2 — JCB 516-40 (Compact Telehandler)

*Read [Module 04 — Telehandlers](../04-telehandlers-and-loaders.md) and the [JCB 541-70 page](C1-jcb-541-70-telehandler.md)
first — the same telehandler rules apply. This page covers what's different about the compact 516-40.*

> Same legal position as the 541-70: **private land only**, trained/competent/supervised, **not on
> a public road** for a 16-year-old.

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | **Compact** telehandler |
| **Max lift capacity** | **~1,600 kg (1.6 t)** — through the full 4 m height |
| **Max lift height** | **~4.0 m** |
| Capacity at reach | **~1,000 kg at ~2 m forward reach** |
| Machine weight | ~3.4 t |

**Why "compact" matters for safety:** it's smaller, lighter and lifts **less than half** what the
541-70 does, and only ~4 m high. It's handy in tight yards and lower buildings — but because it's
light, it can feel **tippy** with a load near its limit. **Don't treat it like the big machine** —
its limits come sooner.

---

## Pre-start daily checks

Same as the [541-70](C1-jcb-541-70-telehandler.md): tyres, boom/hoses/rams, **attachment locked**,
**load chart**, brakes/steering/alarm/beacon/horn/lights, **seatbelt**, correct attachment.

---

## Safe operation — the differences

- **Lower limits:** max ~1.6 t and ~4 m. Round/square bales of haylage are **heavy and wet** — check
  the bale weight against the chart; the 516-40 will reach its limit faster than the 541-70.
- **Lighter machine = easier to tip:** carry the load **low**, travel/turn **slowly**, and be extra
  careful with the load raised or on a slope.
- **Tight spaces:** great for low/old buildings, but mind the **boom hitting beams/doorways** and
  people in confined yard areas — use the alarm/horn and go slow.
- Everything else — load chart, exclusion zone, overhead lines, no lifting people on bare forks,
  reversing awareness — is **exactly as the 541-70 page**.

---

## Fuelling
Engine off, Safe Stop, no smoking. Diesel (+ AdBlue if fitted). See [A1](A1-fuelling-up.md).

## Greasing & operator maintenance
Safe Stop, **boom down**, key in pocket. Grease **boom pivot, ram pins, headstock/quick-hitch, axle
pivots**. Check hydraulic level, leaks, tyres, load chart legible. See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

## Shutdown / parking
Firm level ground, attachment flat on the ground, boom retracted and down, handbrake on, engine off,
key out.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Respect the lower 1.6 t / 4 m limits | Assume it lifts like the 541-70 |
| Check heavy haylage bales against the chart | Pick up a bale heavier than the chart allows |
| Carry low, turn slowly (it's light & tippy) | Rush with a raised load |
| Mind the boom in low buildings/doorways | Clout beams or people in tight yards |
