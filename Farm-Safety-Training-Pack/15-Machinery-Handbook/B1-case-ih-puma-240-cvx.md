# B1 — Case IH Puma 240 CVX (Tractor)

*Read [Module 03 — Tractors](../03-tractors.md) first. This page adds the points specific to the
Puma 240 CVX, our biggest tractor.*

---

## At a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| Type | High-horsepower tractor, ~240 hp |
| Transmission | **CVX = continuously variable transmission (CVT)** — no gears to change; speed is stepless via the throttle/drive lever |
| Fuel | Diesel **+ AdBlue/DEF** (blue cap) — see [A1](A1-fuelling-up.md) |
| Why extra care | It's heavy, powerful and wide — big PTO machines (baler, mowers), heavy draft, and more momentum than the 6150R |

> The CVX has no clutch pedal to "stall" it — it can creep or surge if you're heavy on the drive
> control. Learn the controls **stationary, in a safe space**, before working with it.

## Pre-start daily checks

Do the [A2 daily checks](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md), plus on the Puma:

- [ ] Diesel **and AdBlue** levels.
- [ ] CVX transmission / hydraulic oil level (sight glass).
- [ ] Front linkage/PTO (if fitted, for the front mower) — pins, guard, hoses.
- [ ] Cab clean, **seatbelt** working, fire extinguisher present.
- [ ] Wheel weights / ballast and tyre pressures suit the job (heavy draft work).

## Safe operation

**Start-up**
- Get in using **three points of contact**; settle the **seat suspension** to your weight.
- **Seatbelt on.** Start only from the seat, drive lever in neutral/park, PTO off.
- Let it warm up; watch the dash for warnings (AdBlue/DEF, hydraulics, engine).

**Driving the CVT**
- Move off **gently** — the CVX responds smoothly but instantly; no gear changes, so small inputs
  make big differences at first.
- Set a **speed limit / cruise** appropriate to the yard, field or road and stick to it.
- Use the **handbrake/park** every time you stop — don't rely on the transmission holding it.

**In work (PTO machines)**
- Match **PTO speed** (540/1000) to the implement — wrong speed damages kit and is dangerous.
- Engage PTO at **low revs**, build up gently. Never engage at full throttle.
- Keep everyone clear of the PTO shaft and implement (Module 05).

**Transport / road**
- Latch the **brake pedals together** for the road.
- Mind the **width and length** with mounted/towed kit, especially in our gateways (see field map).
- Beacon on; obey the tractor-on-road rules in Module 03 (licence, width limits at 16).

**Slopes**
- Heavy + high loader/load = higher tipping risk. Up/down not across, slow, no sharp turns
  (Module 03). Take extra care on Sands Hill, The Hill and banky ground.

## Fuelling & AdBlue
Engine off, Safe Stop, no smoking. Diesel in the standard filler; **AdBlue only in the blue filler**.
Don't run the AdBlue tank dry — the engine may de-rate. See [A1](A1-fuelling-up.md).

## Greasing & operator maintenance
Safe Stop, key in pocket. Grease the **front and rear linkage pins, top link, pickup hitch, loader
pins (if loader fitted), and both ends + sliding tube of any PTO shaft** in use. Check oil, coolant
(cold only), hydraulic level, tyres and that all guards are on. See [A2](A2-greasing-and-daily-maintenance.md).

## Shutdown / parking
Firm level ground, lower linkage/loader to the ground, drive lever to neutral/park, **handbrake on,
engine off, key out.** Don't leave it running unattended.

---

## Quick do / don't

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Seatbelt on, learn CVT controls stationary first | Move off sharply / surge in the yard |
| Handbrake every stop | Rely on the transmission to hold on a slope |
| Match & gently engage PTO speed | Slam the PTO in at full revs |
| AdBlue in blue filler only | Put AdBlue in diesel tank (or vice-versa) |
| Up/down slopes, slow | Turn across a slope with a high load |
