# ⚠️ EMERGENCY INFORMATION CARD — Truleigh Manor Farm

*Print this, laminate it, and keep a copy in every tractor/telehandler cab and in the trainee's
pocket. In an emergency, calm and clear information saves minutes — and minutes save lives.*

---

## IN AN EMERGENCY

1. **Make it safe** — Safe Stop machines; don't become a second casualty (electricity, slurry gas,
   machinery).
2. **Check the casualty** — responsive? breathing?
3. **CALL 999** (or **112**) — ask for **AMBULANCE** (and Fire/Police if needed).
4. **Give first aid** within your training.
5. **Send someone to the gate to guide the ambulance in.**

**If someone is caught in machinery:** Safe Stop / switch off power, call 999, control bleeding, keep
any severed part clean & cool with the casualty. **Power line strike:** stay in the cab, drive clear
if you can, keep everyone back, call 999 + 105. **Slurry gas / collapse:** do NOT enter — call 999
and keep clear.
