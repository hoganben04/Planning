# Module 01 — Farm Induction & Golden Rules

*Do this on day one, on site, walking the actual farm with your supervisor.*

---

## Welcome — why this matters

Farming is one of the **most dangerous jobs in the UK**. Agriculture has a fatal injury rate around **21 times higher** than the average across all industries. Most people who are killed or seriously hurt were doing a routine job they'd done many times. The difference between a normal day and a disaster is usually one rushed decision, one skipped check, or one moment standing in the wrong place.

You are 16. You are legally allowed to do this work, but you are new to it. **There is no job on this farm so urgent that it's worth your life or your hands.** If you are ever unsure, **stop and ask.** Nobody will think less of you for asking — but everybody will care if you get hurt.

---

## Your right to a safe day

- You can **say no** to a task you haven't been trained for.
- You can **stop work** if you think something is unsafe.
- You can **ask questions** as many times as you need.
- You will be **supervised** until you are signed off as competent — that is normal and required.

## The Golden Rules (non-negotiable)

These apply to everyone, every day, no exceptions:

1. **SAFE STOP before you leave the seat or anyone approaches.** Handbrake on → controls in neutral → engine off → key out. (Full detail in Module 02.)
2. **Never clear a blockage, adjust or touch a machine while the engine is running or the PTO is turning.** Stop it. Wait for ALL moving parts to stop.
3. **No passengers** on tractors, telehandlers or in/on trailers unless there is a proper, fitted instructor seat. Never ride on a load, drawbar, linkage or in a raised bucket.
4. **Never go under a raised load, raised trailer body, or raised loader/forks** that isn't physically propped/locked. Hydraulics can fail.
5. **Stay clear of the PTO shaft** and all guarded moving parts. Tie up loose clothing, no dangling cords, no loose long hair, no jewellery.
6. **Wear the right PPE** for the job: steel-toe boots always; ear and dust protection in noise/dust; gloves, eye protection and hi-vis as needed.
7. **Mind overhead power lines** — telehandlers, tipping trailers and high loads can reach them. Know where they are.
8. **Don't use a machine you haven't been trained and signed off on.** Ask.
9. **Phone:** never use it while driving/operating. Pull over, Safe Stop, then use it.
10. **If you're tired, ill, or it's getting dark and you're rushing — stop.** Fatigue and "just one more load" cause accidents. Tell your supervisor.
11. **No alcohol or drugs.** They have no place anywhere near this machinery.
12. **If in doubt — STOP and ASK.**
