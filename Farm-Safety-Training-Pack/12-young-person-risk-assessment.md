# Module 12 — Young Person Risk Assessment (Template)

*Complete this **before** the young person starts work. It is a legal requirement under the Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 (reg. 19) and must take account of the young person's inexperience, immaturity and lack of awareness of risk. Review it whenever tasks, equipment or the worker's experience change.*

## How to use the grid

For each hazard: rate the **risk before controls** (Low / Med / High), list the **controls** you will actually put in place, then rate the **residual risk**. Add anything specific to your farm. The "young person factors" column is the legal extra you must consider — their inexperience and how you'll manage it.

## Working hours for under-18s (note)

Young workers (school-leaving age to 18) generally have limits on working hours and rest breaks under the Working Time Regulations (typically not more than 8 hours/day or 40/week, with daily and weekly rest), with limited exceptions in agriculture. **Check current rules and agree reasonable hours** — especially important during pressured haymaking. Fatigue is itself a major hazard (row 18).
