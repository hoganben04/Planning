# Sources & Further Reading

This training pack is built on current UK Health and Safety Executive (HSE) guidance and recognised agricultural good practice. The employer should use the original HSE materials alongside this pack and carry out a site-specific risk assessment.

## Key UK law referenced
- Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974
- Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999 (regulation 19 — young persons)
- Prevention of Accidents to Children in Agriculture Regulations 1998
- Provision and Use of Work Equipment Regulations 1998 (PUWER)
- Lifting Operations and Lifting Equipment Regulations 1998 (LOLER)
- Reporting of Injuries, Diseases and Dangerous Occurrences Regulations 2013 (RIDDOR)
- Working Time Regulations 1998 (limits for under-18s)

## HSE guidance (agriculture)
- HSE — Children and public safety on farms: https://www.hse.gov.uk/agriculture/topics/children.htm
- HSE — Training, supervision and assessing risks to young people: https://www.hse.gov.uk/agriculture/experience/young-people-training.htm
- HSE — Preventing accidents to children on farms (INDG472): https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/indg472.htm
- HSE — Preventing accidents to children in agriculture (L116)
- HSE — Safe workplace transport in agriculture: https://www.hse.gov.uk/agriculture/topics/machinery/farm-vehicles-1.htm
- HSE — Safe maintenance / Safe Stop: https://www.hse.gov.uk/agriculture/topics/maintenance-1.htm
- HSE — Working safely with agricultural machinery (INDG241): https://www.hse.gov.uk/pubns/indg241.pdf
- HSE — Agriculture fatal injury statistics (annual)
